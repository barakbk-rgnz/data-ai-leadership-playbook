# 30/60/90 plan: Head of Data & AI

## Days 1 to 30: Listen and map
- Meet every major stakeholder, ask what decision they cannot make today
- Inventory data assets, dashboards, owners and costs
- Identify the 3 metrics everyone argues about
- Deliver: current-state map and a one-page problem statement

## Days 31 to 60: Pick and prove
- Choose 1 domain for a governed metric layer
- Establish intake and prioritization
- Ship one visible quick win
- Deliver: metric contracts for the first domain, intake process live

## Days 61 to 90: Scale and communicate
- Publish the operating model and service tiers
- Set AI governance basics (use-case intake, spend visibility)
- Agree the next two quarters of roadmap with the exec team
- Deliver: roadmap, scorecard baseline, first QBR
