# AI use-case intake

1. Business problem and the decision it supports
2. Requestor and accountable owner
3. Data used (and sensitivity tier)
4. Expected value (time saved, revenue, risk reduced) and how it will be measured
5. Failure cost: what happens when the AI is wrong?
6. Human in the loop? Where?
7. Risk tier: Low (internal, reversible) / Medium (customer-adjacent) / High (financial, legal, people decisions)
8. Eval plan: golden set, acceptance threshold
9. Run cost estimate (seats, tokens, infra)
10. Decision: Approve / Pilot / Decline, with reason
