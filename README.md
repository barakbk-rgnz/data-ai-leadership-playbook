# Data & AI Leadership Playbook

Practical patterns for running a small, high-leverage data and AI team. Written from hands-on experience leading BI, data engineering, analytics and AI agents in a B2B SaaS environment. Everything here is generic. No employer data, schemas or internal logic.

**The problem it solves:** small data teams drown in ad hoc requests, ship metrics nobody trusts, and get pushed into AI projects before the foundations exist. This playbook is a set of opinionated defaults for avoiding that.

## Who this is for
- Heads of Data and Data & AI leads running teams of 2 to 15
- Leaders inheriting a messy BI estate
- Execs deciding where AI belongs in the data stack
