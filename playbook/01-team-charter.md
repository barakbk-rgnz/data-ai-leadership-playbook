# 1. Team charter and scope

> Draft: sample content, to be refined.

A charter is the one page you point at when someone asks "can the data team just...". Without it, scope is set by whoever shouts loudest.

## What the charter answers
| Question | Example answer |
|---|---|
| Why do we exist? | Help the business make faster, trusted decisions with data and AI |
| Who do we serve? | Exec team, Finance, RevOps, Product, Customer Success |
| What do we own? | Warehouse, semantic layer, certified dashboards, AI agents over governed data |
| What do we not own? | Source system admin, one-off spreadsheet requests, vendor tool configuration |
| How do we measure success? | Decision latency, trust score, adoption of certified assets, cost per insight |

## Sample charter (team of 6)
**Mission:** One trusted version of the numbers that run the business, and AI that sits on top of it.

**Services**
1. Certified metrics and dashboards (Tier 1)
2. Self-serve datasets for analysts in business teams (Tier 2)
3. Ad hoc analysis, time-boxed (Tier 3)
4. AI agents and automations over governed data

**Out of scope**
- Building reports directly on source systems
- Data entry or data cleanup inside source tools
- "Quick" dashboards with no named owner

**Team shape**
| Role | Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Head of Data & AI | 1 | Strategy, stakeholders, governance |
| Analytics engineer | 2 | Models, semantic layer, tests |
| Data engineer | 1 | Ingestion, platform, cost |
| BI / analyst | 1 | Dashboards, enablement |
| AI engineer | 1 | Agents, evals, automations |

## Anti-patterns
- A charter nobody outside the team has read
- Listing tools instead of outcomes
- Promising every team a dedicated analyst
