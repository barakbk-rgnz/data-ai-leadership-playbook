# 11. Stakeholder management for data leaders

> Draft: sample content, to be refined.

Data teams rarely fail on tech. They fail on expectations.

## Map your stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Wants | Fears | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO | One version of the truth for the board | Surprises in front of investors | Monthly 1:1 |
| CFO | Reconciled revenue numbers | Numbers that differ from finance systems | Bi-weekly |
| CRO / RevOps | Pipeline and forecast visibility | Slow answers during quarter end | Weekly |
| CPO | Usage and adoption insight | Data team as a bottleneck | Bi-weekly |
| CS lead | Churn early warning | Another dashboard nobody opens | Monthly |

## Habits that build credibility
- Lead with the decision, not the dashboard
- Say no with a reason and an alternative ("not now, here is when")
- Pre-brief execs before a number changes in a board meeting
- Publish a short monthly update: shipped, next, risks, asks

## Sample monthly update
**Shipped:** Revenue metrics certified, board pack automated (saves Finance 2 days/month)
**Next:** Churn risk model pilot with CS
**Risks:** Active customer definition still disputed, escalated to COO
**Asks:** CS lead to own the churn metric contract
