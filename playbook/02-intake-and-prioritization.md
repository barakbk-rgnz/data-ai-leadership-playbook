# 2. Intake and prioritization

> Draft: sample content, to be refined.

Rule one: every request goes through one door. Slack DMs are not a queue.

## The single front door
- One form or ticket type for all requests
- Required fields: business question, decision it supports, deadline and why, requestor, accountable owner
- Requests without a decision attached go back to the requestor

## Scoring model
Score each request 1 to 5 on four factors, then multiply impact and confidence, divide by effort.

| Factor | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | Nice to know | Changes a revenue or cost decision |
| Reach | One person | Exec team or whole company |
| Confidence | Gut feeling | Clear owner, data exists |
| Effort | Under a day | Over a month |

**Priority = (Impact x Reach x Confidence) / Effort**

## Sample backlog
| Request | Impact | Reach | Conf. | Effort | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Churn risk dashboard for CS | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 26.7 |
| Board pack automation | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 25.0 |
| Marketing attribution v2 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 4.8 |
| Ad hoc: webinar attendee list | 1 | 1 | 5 | 1 | 5.0 |

## Capacity split (sample)
- 60% roadmap (scored work)
- 20% run and maintain
- 20% ad hoc, time-boxed

## Cadence
- Weekly: triage new requests (30 min)
- Monthly: review roadmap with business leads
- Quarterly: reset capacity split based on actuals
