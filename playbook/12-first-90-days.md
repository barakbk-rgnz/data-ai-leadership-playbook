# 12. First 30/60/90 days

> Draft: sample content, to be refined. Template: [first-90-days.md](../templates/first-90-days.md).

The first 90 days decide whether you are seen as a strategic leader or a ticket queue.

## Principles
- Listen before you rebuild. The mess usually has reasons
- Win trust with one visible fix, not a big plan
- Write things down. A one-page current-state map beats ten meetings

## Sample stakeholder interview questions
1. What decision do you struggle to make today?
2. Which number do you not trust, and why?
3. What did the data team do well before I arrived?
4. If I fixed one thing in 90 days, what should it be?

## Sample outputs by day 90
| Output | Audience | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Current-state map | Exec team | Reviewed in leadership meeting |
| Top 3 disputed metrics | Exec team | Owners named for each |
| Intake process | Whole company | All requests go through one door |
| First certified domain | Finance, RevOps | Metric contracts signed |
| Roadmap for next 2 quarters | Exec team | Agreed and published |

## Red flags to surface early
- No exec sponsor for data
- Key pipelines owned by one person
- AI projects already promised externally with no data foundation
