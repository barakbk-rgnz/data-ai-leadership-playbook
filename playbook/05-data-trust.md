# 5. Data trust scorecard

> Draft: sample content, to be refined. Template: [data-trust-scorecard.md](../templates/data-trust-scorecard.md).

Trust is the product. Measure it like one.

## Five dimensions
| Dimension | Question | Sample measure |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Are the numbers right? | % of Tier 1 tests passing |
| Freshness | Are they on time? | % of SLA windows met |
| Consistency | Do sources agree? | # of reconciliation breaks |
| Ownership | Does someone answer for it? | % of Tier 1 assets with named owner |
| Perception | Do people believe it? | Quarterly survey, 1 to 5 |

## Sample quarterly scorecard
| Dimension | Q1 | Q2 | Target | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | 88% | 95% | 98% | Improving |
| Freshness | 91% | 96% | 97% | Improving |
| Consistency (breaks) | 14 | 6 | Under 3 | Improving |
| Ownership | 60% | 85% | 100% | Improving |
| Perception | 2.9 | 3.4 | 4.0 | Improving |

## How to use it
- Publish it. Hiding a bad score destroys more trust than the score
- Tie each red cell to one action with an owner and a date
- Survey the same 20 to 30 stakeholders each quarter so the trend means something
